Skylight Sync Setup
  1. Open the Skylight app on your phone
  2. Go to Settings → Calendar Sources
  3. Tap Add Google Calendar and sign in with the same Google account you connected above
  4. Select the calendar you chose above and tap Sync
  5. Events added by this applet will appear on your Skylight frame within minutes
